## Adding yourself

1. Fork `DilumSaluka/SriLankan-Defense-Power`
2. Create a branch, make your change (follow `SECURITY_CHECKLIST.md` for any interactive feature)
3. Open a PR — please include the source URL for any factual change

All facts on the site are **public record** — no classified or operational data is accepted.
